\chapter{2002 Nobel Prize in Economics}
\textbf{Laureates: }Daniel Kahneman (Israel/USA), Vernon Smith (USA)

\section*{Reason for Award}
Behavioral economics and experimental economics

\section{What They Did}
Daniel Kahneman together with Amos Tversky developed prospect theory replacing expected utility model demonstrating systematic violations of rational choice principles Kahneman showed individuals overweight small probabilities loss aversion effects framing decisions mental accounting procedures Kahneman identified numerous cognitive biases heuristics availability representativeness anchoring effects Vernon Smith established laboratory experimentation as valid economic methodology constructing controlled environments testing market behaviors Smith demonstrated competitive markets achieve efficient outcomes even with limited information frictions His experiments validated theoretical predictions discovering deviations revealing systematic patterns informing behavioral modifications Together Kahneman Smith transformed economics incorporating psychological realism about human decision-making processes validating experimental methods as legitimate research tool complementing theoretical empirical approaches
