\chapter{1987 Nobel Prize in Economics}
\textbf{Laureates: }Robert Solow (USA)

\section*{Reason for Award}
Contributions theory economic growth

\section{What They Did}
Robert Solow developed exogenous growth model showing capital accumulation technological progress drive long-run productivity growth identified Solow residual measuring unexplained technological progress after accounting for capital and labor contributions His neoclassical growth model demonstrated conditional convergence poorer economies catch up richer ones if save rates technologies similar Showed steady-state growth requires exogenous technological advancement Solow model became standard teaching tool for growth economics presenting intuitive graphical analysis capital accumulation diminishing returns transitional dynamics Long-run growth driven by technological progress not mere accumulation Solow model provided benchmark against which endogenous growth theories later compared

\section{Impact on Economic Thinking}
Solow transformed growth economics from philosophical speculation quantitative analysis Providing framework where capital accumulation contributes growth but eventually diminishing returns limit effect Technological progress essential sustained growth His identification of residual attributed technological change became standard growth accounting procedure Conditional convergence hypothesis explained pattern cross-country incomes poorer nations tend grow faster catching up rich ones when saving rates institutions similar His model demonstrated importance of savings rate investment in capital formation while emphasizing ultimate dependency on technological advancement Growth accounting exercises worldwide decompose growth into capital labor total productivity components following Solow methodology

\section{Modern Perspectives Today}
Endogenous growth theories emerged challenging Solow exogenous technological progress assumption internalizing innovation knowledge spillovers human capital accumulation Yet Solow model remains fundamental benchmark growth analysis Teaching introductory macroeconomics Condensed growth accounting uses Solow decomposition Empirical studies testing convergence hypotheses use Solow framework as starting point Policy makers evaluating growth strategies reference Solow conclusions about importance technological progress investment education Research extending Solow model incorporating environmental sustainability demographic transition climate change reflects Solow enduring relevance His basic insights remain correct though enriched by deeper understanding technological determination Solow framework taught universally as foundation advanced growth theory.
