\section{Impact on Economic Thinking}
Hicks and Arrow together, strengthened theoretical foundations establishing rigorous proofs market equilibrium existence clarifying limits collective decision-making Hicks simplified complex Keynesian dynamics tractable analytical form enabling clearer understanding fiscal monetary policy interactions His general equilibrium approach provided coherence between microeconomic behavior macroeconomic outcomes Arrow's proof of general equilibrium existence validated theoretical coherence market mechanisms simultaneously identifying potential failure points through social choice limitations Together these contributions deepened economists understanding market coordination efficiency properties institutional constraints influencing both theoretical research practical policy design
