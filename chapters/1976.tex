\textbf{Laureates: }Milton Friedman (USA)

\section*{Reason for Award}
Consumption analysis monetary history stabilization policy

\section{What They Did}
Milton Friedman championed free-market capitalism developing permanent income hypothesis proposing consumption depends expected lifetime income rather than current income provided historical analysis money s role economic fluctuations challenging Keynesian orthodoxy emphasizing quantity theory of money demonstrating inflation always monetary phenomenon requiring stable monetary growth rules

\section{Impact on Economic Thinking}
Friedman's reinvigorated classical monetary theory arguing monetary policy primary determinant nominal economic fluctuations rejecting active fiscal stabilization advocating single rule for monetary growth influencing central bank policies worldwide Monetarist critique inspired deregulation movements skepticism government intervention emphasizing individual freedom market solutions Permanent income hypothesis reshaped consumption-savings analysis linking macroeconomic stability microeconomic decisions
