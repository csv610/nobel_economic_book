\chapter{1983 Nobel Prize in Economics}
\textbf{Laureates: }Gérard Debreu (France/USA)

\section*{Reason for Award}
Rigorous reformulation general equilibrium theory.
\section{What They Did}
Gérard Debreu provided mathematical proof existence competitive equilibrium using convex set theory transforming general equilibrium into mathematically rigorous discipline His seminal work Theory of Value established general equilibrium theory proper mathematical foundations utilizing topological fixed-point theorems existence of equilibrium under well-defined assumptions Demonstrated existence convex preferences continuous functions well-behaved production sets Debreu contributed to utility theory proving existence continuous utility function representing ordinal preferences provided rigorous treatment of arbitrage-free pricing in complete markets

\section{Impact on Economic Thinking}
Debreu s work transformed general equilibrium from heuristic reasoning into rigorous mathematical discipline enabling precise statements about existence uniqueness stability of equilibria His formulation of arbitrage-free pricing provided foundation for modern asset pricing theory By establishing general equilibrium under convexity conditions he clarified when markets achieve Pareto optimality giving precise conditions under which competitive allocations are socially optimal His mathematical approach influenced generations of theorists who extend his framework to incomplete markets dynamic settings international trade

\section{Modern Perspectives Today}
General equilibrium theory remains cornerstone theoretical economics with Debreu s formulation providing essential mathematical tools Existence uniqueness stability analyses build upon his foundational work Asset pricing theory relies on his arbitrage-free characterization Extensions to incomplete markets dynamic general equilibrium search frictions heterogeneous agents all depend on mathematical clarity Debreu brought Precision economics allowing researchers explore complex questions about market failures public goods externalities with rigorous methodology His work underpins much of contemporary microeconomic theory and continues guide research directions
