\chapter{1973 Nobel Prize in Economics}
\textbf{Laureates: }Wassily Leontief (USA)

\section*{Reason for Award}
Development of input-output analysis.

\section{What They Did}
Leontief created input-output analysis representing interdependencies different economy sectors matrices recording flow goods services producers consumers organizing information coefficients tables capturing direct requirements per unit output enabled systematic analysis how changes final demand ripple through entire economy affecting employment prices productivity resource allocation He constructed first comprehensive input-output table US economy 1941 demonstrating practical applicability

\section{Impact on Economic Thinking}
Input-output analysis provided earliest comprehensive quantitative framework understanding economic interdependence systemic effects Before Leontief examination tended individual markets isolation analysis demonstrated shocks propagate across interconnected sectors fundamentally changing understanding economic structure vulnerability The technique became indispensable regional economics urban planning environmental analysis calculating pollution intensity sector technological forecasting international trade studies Government agencies worldwide adopted input-output tables standard analytical tools economic planning budgetary assessment impact evaluation Leontief s approach established principle economies constitute integrated systems requiring coordinated analysis collection separate parts
