\chapter{1994 Nobel Prize in Economics}
\textbf{Laureates: }John Nash (USA), Reinhard Selten (Germany), John Harsanyi (Hungary/USA)

\section*{Reason for Award}
Non-cooperative game theory.
\section{What They Did}
John Nash introduced equilibrium concept defining situation players strategies best responses simultaneously determining mutually consistent outcomes Nash equilibrium became foundational solution concept non-cooperative games Reinhard Selten refined equilibrium concept introducing subgame perfection eliminating implausible equilibria in dynamic sequential games emphasizing credible threats John Harsanyi extended game theory to incomplete information situations where players possess private knowledge transforming analysis auctions bargaining negotiations Under incomplete information assumption each player type represents distinct private information set Harsanyi common prior assumption enabling Bayesian updating Strategies based types produce Bayesian Nash equilibrium Three laureates together founded modern game theory providing rigorous mathematical framework analyzing strategic interactions across disciplines game theoretic methods now standard economics political science biology computer science philosophy international relations military strategy bargaining theory auction design mechanism design all build upon foundations laid by Nash Selten Harsanyi
