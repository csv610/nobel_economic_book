\textbf{Laureates: }Gary Becker (USA)

\section*{Reason for Award}
Extension microeconomic analysis family crime discrimination

\section{What They Did}
Gary Becker applied economic analysis to sociological phenomena including crime education fertility family discrimination addiction time allocation in households Developed human capital theory treating education investment as productive expenditure with measurable returns Extended labor economics incorporating household production theory analyzing allocation of time between market work leisure domestic activity Created economic model of crime treating criminal behavior utility maximization considering probabilities punishment benefits Rational addiction model framing drug use dynamic optimization problem where current consumption depends expectations future costs Benefits Analysis of discrimination showing it irrational profit-maximizing employers under competitive conditions discrimination persists due to prejudice or institutional barriers rather than economic advantage Becker developed penetrating analysis fertility decisions incorporating opportunity costs children quantity quality tradeoff Parental investment theory explaining human capital accumulation in children Becker s interdisciplinary approach expanded economic methodology domains previously considered outside economics realm
